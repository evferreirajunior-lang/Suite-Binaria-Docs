Suite Binária v0.02 — Changelog

Highlights

v0.02 consolidates the development line that followed v0.01 and promotes to release the fixes that passed global regression testing and heavy audits.

Wild Encounter

  • strengthened resolution of repointed/expanded encounter tables;
  • more robust map catalog without replacing the conservative path used by vanilla ROMs and common ROM hacks;
  • creation, removal, and editing of existing areas with additional validation;
  • support for maps without a Wild header when safe creation is possible;
  • controlled recovery of invalid/configured structures;
  • performance and navigation improvements;
  • Ctrl+F search;
  • geometry fixes to keep the grid stable with large lists and long Pokémon names.

PokéAPI

  • review of the Base Stats, Evolutions, Learnsets, Egg Moves, and Pokédex interfaces;
  • window-size persistence and responsive defaults;
  • resizable windows with consistent behavior;
  • layout, navigation, Auto-Apply/Auto-Save, and preservation improvements;
  • stronger aliases, cache, and operation-state handling.

Editors and Gen III compatibility

  • stronger explicit support for Emerald, FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, and Sapphire;
  • fixes and audits in Pokémon, Move, Item, Ability, Trainer, and Trade Editor;
  • improvements to repointing, expanded tables, stale-check, and rollback;
  • internationalization and language-catalog refinements.

Quality and safety

  • global shard-based regression across the five canonical vanilla ROMs;
  • additional testing on an expanded ROM hack without inheriting exceptional rules from other ROMs;
  • profile/signature dispatch validation;
  • fault-injection, import, compilation, and writer-integrity tests;
  • architectural fixes identified by heavy audits.

Compatibility

v0.02 keeps explicit profiles for the five vanilla Rev 0 ROMs. ROM hacks may alter internal structures and must be tested individually; the Suite does not automatically treat a ROM hack as equivalent to vanilla based only on its Game Code.
